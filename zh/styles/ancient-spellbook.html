This is a millennia-old hero's grimoire, recording lost runic incantations and ancient ritual secrets. Its pages have yellowed and curled at the edges, the leather cover inscribed with long-forgotten scripts, shimmering with mysterious golden light between the lines.

When moonlight falls upon the cover, the inscriptions begin to breathe — flickering like a heartbeat. Each page is crafted from fine parchment, the ink mixed with dragon's blood and stardust powder, ensuring the words never fade. After a thousand years, magic still flows through these pages.

Legend says only the chosen hero can decipher its mysteries. Those who turn these pages shall receive the blessing of the ancient guardians — the triple boon of Wisdom, Strength, and Magic.
