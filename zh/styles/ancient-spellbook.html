The origin of Futhark and the hero's destiny

The Allfather's Sacrifice

Odin, the Allfather, hung from the World Tree Yggdrasil for nine days and nights, sacrificing his right eye to glimpse the secrets of the runes. He bestowed them upon heroes, that they may find light in the darkness.

The Chosen Hero

只有The Chosen Hero才能解读书中奥秘。翻开书页的人，将获得远古守护者的加护——Wisdom、Strength与勇气的三重祝福。勇者之路并非坦途，但符文之光永不熄灭。
